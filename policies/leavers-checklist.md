# Leavers checklist

Do this on the person's last working day (sooner if they leave on bad terms).

- [ ] **Block sign-in** for their account in Microsoft Entra, so it can't be used even if the password is known.
- [ ] **Revoke active sessions**, so any devices already signed in are logged out.
- [ ] **Remove them from groups** such as All Staff, and from any shared mailboxes.
- [ ] **Move what the business needs.** Hand their mailbox and files to their manager, and set an out-of-office or forward if needed.
- [ ] **Remove the licence last**, once the data is safe, and delete the account after your retention period.

*How this was tested in the lab:* Ed was the simulated leaver. His sign-in was blocked, his sessions revoked, his licence removed and he was taken out of All Staff.
